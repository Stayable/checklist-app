1a Deviations from Elevate · decisions · token map
Three deliberate deviations
1 · Field phone surfaces raise contrast two stops
Elevate's --se-ink-3 on --se-cream is 4.0:1 — under AA and invisible in Florida sun. On 1b/1c/1d all body and meta text is --se-ink (15.2:1) or --se-ink-2 (9.4:1); ink-3 appears only as a non-text hairline label colour on desk surfaces. New tokens: --se-field-text, --se-field-control (#8A8478 — control borders hold up at low screen brightness), --se-field-ground (#F6F1E6, one stop deeper than cream so a paper card still separates from the page), --se-tap-min: 44px. Warmth kept; low contrast dropped.
2 · Manager tables sit on paper, not cream
Cream ground + oat hairlines at 30 rows turns to mush. So: the table is a --se-paper plate on a cream page, zebra at 1.2% (--se-zebra #FCF9F2) rather than linen, one --se-border-strong outer edge, vertical column rules at --se-col-rule only between number columns where misreading costs something. Hover is sky-derived (--se-row-hover) so it can never be mistaken for a status wash. Column rhythm is fixed-width for every bounded value (status, code, counts, time) and fluid for the two text columns — that is what stops the overflow in 1f.
3 · Print keeps a white page; warmth lives in panels
Cream flooded across Letter bands on a worn laser drum and muddies photocopies. The PDFs print on --se-print-page #FFFFFF; the warm note appears only in the meta panel, table headers and flag washes at --se-print-panel #F4F1E9 (≈3% ink — below the banding threshold, survives a fax). The legacy cool #F4F5F7 is kept as --se-print-panel-cool for any future full-bleed fill. Greyscale: panel → 96% grey, table header → 93%, navy heads → 12% (near-black), sky rule → 55%, warning tint → 94% distinguished by its 1.5pt left rule, danger wash → 93% plus a solid left rule and the literal word. Toggle PDF greyscale in Tweaks to check it.
Smaller calls
Poppins weights: 400 / 600 / 700. 500 is dropped so the PDF pipeline bundles three .ttf files. Eyebrows and buttons use 700, labels and prompts 600, everything else 400. No italics anywhere — hints use size + colour + a leading rule instead.
Sky is the only interactive hue. Coral stays retired and aliased. Sunshine is decorative only: the login panel dots, the wordmark accent, the dashboard sparkline zero-line. Where yellow must be text it is --se-sunshine-text.
Red keeps its meaning. Of the 8 states only Flagged uses danger; Cancelled and Expired are ink + glyph, Submitted is sky, Reviewed is positive. Overdue SLA in the issues report uses weight + a ▲ glyph + the word "overdue", never hue alone.
Asymmetric card radius (14 14 18 14) is kept on cards and sheets, but not on inputs, chips or table plates — at 30 rows the odd corner reads as a rendering bug.
Dark mode: nothing here uses a colour that cannot invert. Sky-as-text flips to --se-sky-2 lighter; navy surfaces lift rather than sink. Toggle Dark component sheet in Tweaks.
Noted, not moved
IA, routes, columns, field names, status names, role names, property codes and the Closed / Flag verbs are untouched.
Observation (no change made): the review queue's Time to complete and Photo count read as a pair; they are adjacent here but still their own columns. Moving them under one header would be an IA change — your call.
Gap, not invented: nothing in the data distinguishes "overdue" from "Scheduled" on the phone; 1b sorts Sent back first and shows a due time only where the record carries one. No new field drawn.
PDF workarounds: no shadows anywhere (borders carry separation), no grid (every page is nested flex rows with fixed width in pt), header/footer are fixed children, hint rules and flag markers are 1pt bordered views rather than pseudo-elements, thumbnails are fixed-size image boxes in a wrapping row, and the page-number token is rendered by the renderer's own page callback.

Why portrait, and why the bar stays Portrait: seven numeric columns and an 8-row body fit inside 564pt of live width with room to spare, and this report is filed and stapled alongside the checklist exports — a landscape sheet in a portrait stack gets turned sideways and lost. Landscape would only earn its place if a per-week column series were added, which the data does not carry. The 48pt bar beside each percentage is the one chart element: same baseline, one quantity, and the number is printed beside it, so the bar adds scanning order and nothing else. In greyscale the fill reads 12% grey on an 88% track — still a length comparison, and the figures carry the precision.

Density and greyscale Rows alternate in pairs (plain / #FAF8F3) with urgent-or-overdue rows on the deeper #F7F4EE, so the eye finds trouble by band before reading a word. Priority is a single word in four escalating treatments — Low plain, Med hairline box, High solid box, Urgent reversed — a ladder that survives a photocopy with no hue at all, and overdue SLA is marked three ways: a ▲ glyph, bold weight, and the literal "overdue 4d". This page carries no colour by design; it is the one most likely to be faxed.
