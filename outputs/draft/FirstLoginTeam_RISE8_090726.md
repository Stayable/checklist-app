## 1. Sign in

**https://ops.rentstayable.com** — it's a website, not an app. No download.

- **Email:** your work email
- **Password:** `Ops` + your name, capitalised — like **`OpsBea`**

First time only:

1. Email and password.
2. **We email you a 6-digit code.** Check your email, type the code in.
3. **Pick your own password.** At least 8 characters. Don't reuse the one above.

**5 wrong tries locks you out for 30 minutes.** If it won't let you in, stop and
tell your manager.

## 2. Put it on your phone's home screen

Do this once and it opens like a normal app.

- **iPhone:** open it in Safari → **Share** → **Add to Home Screen**
- **Android:** open it in Chrome → **⋮** menu → **Add to Home screen**

## 3. Your checklists

**Home** shows what's assigned to you today. Tap one to open it.

- Answer each question. A **`*`** means you can't submit without it.
- **Photos are taken with the camera, right then.** You can't pick an old photo
  from your gallery.
- Keep **location on** — photos are stamped with where they were taken.
- **Submit** when you're done. Your manager reviews it.

Questions are in **English and Español** — same wording your team already uses.

## Something wrong?

Tell your manager. Wrong question, missing question, photo won't upload, anything.
That's what these first few weeks are for.
